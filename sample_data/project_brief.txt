PROJECT BRIEF

Project Name: Rehabilitation of Metalled Road, Chowk Azam to Kot Sultan
Length: 12.4 km
District: Layyah
Executing Department: Communication & Works (C&W) Department, Government of the Punjab
Administrative Approval: PKR 512.0 million
Approved (Contract) Cost: PKR 486.5 million
Contractor: M/S Al-Barkat Construction Co.
Mode of Award: Competitive bidding, single-stage two-envelope
Date of Commencement: 15 January 2026
Time Allowed for Completion: 10 months (Completion due: 14 November 2026)
Defect Liability Period: 12 months from date of completion

Scope of Work:
- Scarifying and reshaping of existing sub-grade over 12.4 km carriageway (24 ft wide)
- Provision of 6-inch sub-base course (crushed stone)
- Provision of 6-inch water bound macadam (WBM) base course
- Prime coat and tack coat application
- 2-inch asphalt wearing course (hot mix asphalt)
- Reconstruction of 4 RCC box culverts and 2 slab culverts
- Earthen shoulders, 3 ft each side, with turfing
- Road signs, safety markings, and reflective delineators
- Guard rails at 3 identified hazardous curves

Technical Requirements:
- Materials and workmanship as per Punjab C&W Department Standard Specifications, 2021 edition
- Compaction of sub-base and base course to 98% of Modified AASHTO density
- Asphalt wearing course as per Marshall Mix Design, min. stability 1500 lbs
- Camber 2.5% on straight sections; super-elevation on curves as per design drawings

Milestones (per contract schedule):
1. Mobilization and site clearance — Month 1
2. Earthwork and sub-base — Months 1-4
3. Base course (WBM) — Months 3-6
4. Culvert reconstruction — Months 2-6
5. Prime coat, tack coat and wearing course — Months 6-9
6. Shoulders, signage, guard rails, and finishing — Months 8-10

Reporting Requirement: Monthly progress report to the Superintending Engineer, with physical progress percentage, financial progress, and photographs of ongoing works.
