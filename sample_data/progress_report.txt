MONTHLY PROGRESS REPORT

Project: Rehabilitation of Metalled Road, Chowk Azam to Kot Sultan (12.4 km), District Layyah
Contractor: M/S Al-Barkat Construction Co.
Report Period: Month 7 of 10 (as of 25 August 2026)
Submitted To: Superintending Engineer, C&W Circle, Layyah

1. OVERALL PROGRESS
Planned Physical Progress (per contract schedule, cumulative): 72%
Actual Physical Progress (as measured on site): 58%
Financial Progress: PKR 268 million of PKR 486.5 million contract value utilized (55%)

2. ACTIVITY-WISE STATUS
- Mobilization and site clearance: 100% complete
- Earthwork and sub-grade preparation: 100% complete (12.4 km)
- Sub-base (crushed stone): 95% complete
- WBM base course: 70% complete (8.9 of 12.4 km)
- Culvert reconstruction: 2 of 6 culverts complete, 2 in progress, 2 not started
- Prime coat / tack coat: started on completed base course sections only, approx. 3.5 km treated
- Asphalt wearing course: not yet started
- Shoulders, signage, guard rails: not started

3. CONTRACTOR'S REMARKS
Progress on WBM base course was slower than planned in Months 5-6 due to non-availability of crushed stone aggregate from the approved quarry source and two weeks of monsoon rain in July. The asphalt plant has been mobilized but wearing course work could not commence as the base course is not yet ready on the full alignment. Remaining two culverts are delayed pending finalization of stream diversion arrangements with the Irrigation Department.

No reportable safety incidents during the period. Traffic diversion signage at the two active culvert sites reported as installed; not independently verified during report preparation.
